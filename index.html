About APEX

APEX is a physical AI framework in which agentic AI provides contextual reasoning, adaptive planning, and real-time analysis, while humans perform dexterous physical execution. Wearable mixed-reality hardware streams experimental context to the AI and delivers in-situ guidance back to the operator.
