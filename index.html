About APEX

APEX is a physical AI framework in which agentic AI provides contextual reasoning, adaptive planning, and real-time analysis, while humans perform dexterous physical execution. Wearable mixed-reality hardware streams experimental context to the AI and delivers in-situ guidance back to the operator.

Perceive

State-of-the-art mixed-reality goggles capture 8K egocentric video with a 98°–110° field of view at 32 ms latency, plus hand tracking, eye gaze, and SLAM-based 3D maps of the cleanroom — grounding every action in its physical context.

Reason

A hierarchical multi-agent framework: fast, fine-tuned local vision-language models handle safety monitoring and wafer characterization, while high-level Planning, Context, Step-tracking, and Analysis agents interpret long, multi-step fabrications as they unfold.

Collaborate

Real-time 3D overlays deliver stepwise guidance, error alerts, and safety warnings in the operator's view. Protocols and devices co-evolve through two interacting loops: protocol co-development and device co-fabrication.

Six Core Features

APEX runs as an observational collaborator in the cleanroom, combining six capabilities into a single closed loop.

Fabrication Step Tracking

Aligns every mixed-reality frame to its standard operating procedure step using an evolving multimodal memory, tracking full procedures such as reactive-ion etching end to end.

Fabrication Analysis & Log

Autonomously generates structured, timestamped experiment records — parameters, equipment readings, environmental snapshots — that users can query in natural language.

Real-Time Error Correction

Compares physical operations against the protocol to catch wrong parameters, premature steps, and early terminations, reaching 96% average accuracy across representative failure modes.

Defect Characterization & Traceback

Interprets microscope images against CAD designs and fabrication history to identify wafer defects and trace them back to the exact step that caused them — with a <1 s quick-check mode for time-critical etching.

Safety Monitoring

A locally deployed, fine-tuned safety agent watches chemical handling continuously, issuing preventive warnings, danger alerts, and recovery plans within 2 seconds of an unsafe event.

Protocol Co-Development

Reasons over material compatibility, equipment availability, and literature to draft new fabrication protocols with humans — then revises them mid-fabrication as real observations arrive.

Key Results

Understands the cleanroom better than general models

APEX outperformed GPT-5-nano, GPT-4o, Gemini-2.5-flash, and Gemini-2.5-pro at recognizing fabrication tools (average +23%) and tracked multi-step procedures with 51% higher step-tracking accuracy.

Turns novices into experts in hours, not months

APEX-assisted beginners completed reactive-ion etching at speeds statistically indistinguishable from experts — a 113% speedup over manual-guided novices — and task completion rose from 8% to 100%.

Catches errors before they ruin a wafer

Real-time recognition and correction of parameter mistakes, premature chamber openings, and early terminations with 96% average accuracy — plus autonomous, queryable experiment documentation.

Case Study: A Soft Neural Probe, Co-Developed with AI

Elastomers like SEBS match the mechanical softness of brain tissue but are notoriously incompatible with conventional microfabrication. Working in its two closed loops, APEX proposed a release–float–transfer encapsulation strategy, then revised it mid-fabrication with a PDMS-assisted transfer when the free-standing film proved unreliable.

Wafer-scale fabrication of few-micrometer-thick planar SEBS neural probes
Single-unit resolution in vivo neural recordings, stable within one week
